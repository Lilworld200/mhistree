## Hosting, testing and noncommercial clarification

Deploying on Vercel does not by itself establish production use. NVIDIA staff includes prototyping, development, testing, research and learning within trial use. A private Vercel deployment used for internal evaluation with invented patient scenarios is consistent with that description; this is our reading, not NVIDIA approval of a specific deployment. Vercel's own production/preview labels do not settle the provider's permitted purpose. Real operational use, including people relying on records or care, should not be assumed to remain evaluation merely because it earns no money; the trial terms provide no blanket noncommercial exception. The separate restrictions on sensitive/health data apply during testing too, including a developer's own real health information, absent express permission. Owner clarified the present intent is development/testing rather than a major commercial business; no live patient-data permission or provider switch follows from that clarification. Sources: staff explanation and API Trial Terms linked below.
